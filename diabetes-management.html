Our approach

How we support you

01

Strength training

Building muscle gives your body more capacity to use glucose. We progress load gradually and safely.

02

Daily movement

Walking targets, short post-meal movement and low-impact cardio that fit real life.

03

Balanced nutrition

Plate structure, carbohydrate quality and meal timing, kept simple and sustainable.

04

Habit tracking

Steps, sleep, water and stress, reviewed regularly so small wins add up.

Your journey in three steps

01

Assess

WEEK 1. We review your health history, medication timing, current activity and goals before a single rep.

02

Build

WEEKS 2–6. Progressive strength and movement routines, with nutrition adjustments you can actually keep.

03

Sustain

ONGOING. Regular reviews, plan updates and habits that last long after the programme ends.

Our promise

Safety comes first.

  • We ask about medication and symptoms, and plan session timing around them
  • We never advise changing or stopping your medication
  • We watch for signs of low or high blood sugar and adjust the session
  • We encourage you to share progress with your doctor or dietitian

Please note: TFI programmes are fitness and lifestyle support. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and do not replace your doctor. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need my doctor's approval first?

We strongly recommend you check with your doctor before starting, especially if you take medication. We will plan around any guidance you share.

Will this replace my medication or diet plan?

No. Our programmes support your medical care and never replace it. Any change to medication must come from your doctor.

Can I train virtually?

Yes. Virtual and in-person options both work well, and your coach adapts sessions to your schedule and meals.
