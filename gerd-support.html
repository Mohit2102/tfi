Who it's for

Stay active without the discomfort.

Small changes to timing, positions and portions can make training far more comfortable.

Experiencing heartburn or reflux, especially around exercise

Unsure how meal size and timing affect your workouts

Avoiding exercise for fear of discomfort

Under medical care and wanting a supportive routine

Our approach

How we support you

Timing

Planning sessions around meals so you are not training on a full stomach.

Position choices

Low-impact movement that avoids inverted or high-pressure positions that can aggravate reflux.

Meal structure

Smaller, balanced meals and spotting your personal trigger foods.

Breathing & posture

Relaxed breathing and upright posture to reduce abdominal pressure.

Your journey in three steps

Assess

WEEK 1.Symptoms, triggers, meal pattern and goals, so we know what to avoid.

Build

WEEKS 2–6.Comfortable, progressive training with meal adjustments that suit you.

Sustain

ONGOING.Refine your routine as symptoms improve and fitness grows.

Our promise

Safety comes first.

  • We do not diagnose; persistent symptoms need medical attention
  • We stop and refer you if you have chest pain, difficulty swallowing or unexplained weight loss
  • We never adjust your medication
  • We personalise because triggers vary from person to person

Please note: TFI programmes are fitness and lifestyle support. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and do not replace your doctor. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan.

Can I still do core exercises?

Often yes, with modified positions and timing. We choose what feels comfortable for you.

How long after eating should I train?

This varies for everyone. We help you find a timing that works for your body.

Will exercise make my reflux worse?

Some positions and high-pressure efforts can, so we plan around them. Gentle regular activity often helps.
